Paradise Resort

Paradise Resort Vattavada is a serene, family-run nature getaway located in the peaceful farming village of Oorkad, Vattavada, about 40 kilometers from Munnar town. Perfectly catering to group travelers, couples, and outdoor enthusiasts, the property features a highly flexible inventory including a 3-BHK private cottage, group dormitories, and private outdoor adventure tents.
